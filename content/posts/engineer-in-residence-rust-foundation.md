I am excited to put on an additional hat: Engineer in Residence at the Rust Foundation, seconded for a year by AWS.

Rust is in a moment of great opportunity, and I believe that the Rust community can chart a new course for the industry in supply chain security and maintainer sustainability. I'm looking forward to getting my hands dirty working full-time on infrastructure and programs that reach all Rust users.

Besides the colleagues I mentioned in the [linked post](https://rustfoundation.org/media/welcoming-jess-izen-as-engineer-in-residence-at-the-rust-foundation/) from the Rust Foundation, Rust Project, Rust community, and AWS, I want to give a special thank you to open source leaders that have shared a steady stream of advice about how companies like AWS can fit into this story while supporting maintainer autonomy: [Josh Aas](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josh-aas-406a772/), [Benjamin Nickolls](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benjamuk/), [Henri Yandell](https://www.linkedin.com/in/flamefew/), [Alexander Scammon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexscammon/), [George Chellapa Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/georgechellapa/), [Duane O'Brien](https://www.linkedin.com/in/duaneobrien/), [Æva Black](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aevaonline/).
