*This was pulled from my old [LinkedIn](https://lnkd.in/p/gMQytiHC).*

[Predrag Gruevski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/predrag-gruevski-0abb36296/), [Erik Jonkers](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erik-jonkers/), and I are now members of [The Rust Programming Language](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rust-lang/) Project!

We (especially Predrag and Erik) have been working closely with other project members for quite a while in lots of ways, but neat to have it be official. We are part of a new funding-advisors team that helps other project members figure out how to make their work legible to sponsors, and generally switchboards and does whatever we can to build financial sustainability.

One focus for me is finding ways to provide longer time horizon guarantees of funding, for instance multi year contracts or getting people hired on as staff. We have a lot of strong devs that are now starting to have funding, which is good, but it also needs to be something you can plan around in a bigger way.

Stay tuned!
